About

Tools for the work students do every day.

StudentCalc Nepal is a small, independent collection of free calculators for academic planning. The goal is straightforward: help students get a useful answer quickly, and make the calculation behind it easy to understand.

Built for clarity

Our tools cover GPA estimates, attendance, marks, grades, dates, and study planning. Results include the formula used so students can check their inputs and understand what a number means.

Care around grading

Schools, examinations, and universities may use different rules or update them over time. Our NEB and SEE GPA tools use the points and credits entered by each user; they do not claim to reproduce an official result. Confirm grading rules with the institution responsible for your course.

Privacy by default

Calculations currently run in your browser. The site does not require an account, and the calculator entries are not sent to a server.
