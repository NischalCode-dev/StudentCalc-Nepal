About StudentCalc

Clear tools for everyday study decisions.

StudentCalc Nepal is an independent educational website with free calculators and practical study guides for students in Nepal. It was created to make common study calculations easier to check and understand, without requiring an account or a calculator app.

Who the site is for

The tools are intended for secondary-school, SEE, NEB, and university students, as well as anyone who wants to understand these calculations. They support personal learning and planning; they do not replace advice or records from a school, university, or examination authority.

What you can find here

StudentCalc includes tools for GPA and grades, attendance, marks, dates, exam planning, and study time. The study guides explain selected formulas and show worked examples so visitors can understand how a result is produced rather than relying on a number alone.

How calculations are maintained

Calculators are implemented in the site's JavaScript, and their pages describe the formulas and assumptions used by the current implementation. Grading scales, credit hours, subject combinations, and institutional rules may vary or change. NEB and SEE results are estimates based on the assumptions shown on each page; they are not official results. We do not claim affiliation with an examination authority.

We welcome correction reports and suggestions. If you find a possible error or unclear explanation, contact us with the calculator name, example inputs, and expected result. We can review reports and update the site where appropriate; there is no claim of an official review or approval process. Do not send private academic records or other sensitive information.

Privacy and independence

Calculator inputs are processed in your browser and are not submitted to a StudentCalc application server. The site does not require an account. We are not affiliated with NEB, SEE, a university, or a government examination authority. For details about site data and advertising, read our Privacy Policy and Disclaimer.
