Attendance

How to calculate your class attendance

Attendance percentage compares the classes you attended with the classes your institution counts as conducted. It is a useful way to monitor your record, but whether a student meets a requirement depends on the institution's current policy and how it counts classes, periods, labs, or approved absences.

Attendance formula

Attendance percentage = (classes attended / classes conducted) × 100

Missed classes, if needed, are conducted classes - attended classes. Count only sessions according to your school's record; this calculator expects a whole-number attended count no greater than the conducted total.

Worked examples

Example 1: current attendance

  1. A student attended 18 out of 24 conducted classes.
  2. Divide 18 by 24: 18 / 24 = 0.75.
  3. Multiply by 100: attendance is 75%, with 24 - 18 = 6 missed classes.

Example 2: estimate classes to reach an 80% target

  1. Starting from 18 attended out of 24, suppose the student attends every upcoming class.
  2. Set (18 + x) / (24 + x) = 0.80, where x is the number of future classes attended.
  3. 18 + x = 19.2 + 0.8x, so 0.2x = 1.2 and x = 6 classes.
  4. Check: 24 / 30 = 80% after attending those six classes.

Practical attendance tracking

For students in Nepal, compare your own count with the attendance record maintained by your school, college, or university. Ask whether the record counts a day, each period, practical sessions, or only eligible classes. The recovery example assumes every future class is attended and each adds one to both counts; it does not grant permission to miss classes or confirm eligibility for an exam.

Common mistakes

  • Entering absences as the number attended.
  • Counting cancelled classes or holidays when they are not included in the official record.
  • Mixing class days with individual periods or lab sessions.
  • Treating an estimate as proof of meeting a policy without checking the institution's records.

Frequently asked questions

Should cancelled classes count?

Use the rule applied by your institution. Exclude a cancelled session if the official record does not count it as conducted.

How many classes do I need to attend to recover?

It depends on your current attended and conducted totals and the target. For a target p expressed as a fraction, solve (attended + x) / (conducted + x) ≥ p and round x up to a whole class. The recovery calculator performs this estimate.

Does a target percentage represent a national requirement?

No. Enter a target only after confirming the applicable rule with your school or institution.
