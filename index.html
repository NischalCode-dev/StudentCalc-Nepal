A few useful details

Frequently asked questions

Are the calculators free?

Yes. The calculators are available without payment or an account.

Are NEB and SEE GPA results official?

No. The GPA tools use the grade points and credits you enter. Confirm the current grading scale and rules with your school or examination authority.

Are my entries uploaded?

No. Calculations run locally in the browser, and the current version does not submit your entries to a server.

Can I use the tools on my phone?

Yes. Pages adapt to smaller screens and the calculator controls support keyboard and touch input.
