Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

This policy explains how StudentCalc Nepal ("StudentCalc", "we", or "us") handles information when you visit www.kaflenischal1.com.np. The site is a static website designed to work without an account.

Calculator inputs

Calculations run in your browser. The values you enter into the calculators are not submitted to a StudentCalc application server or stored by StudentCalc. The NEB and SEE tools have an optional name field used only to label the estimate printed from your browser; the name is not required for calculation and is not sent to or saved by StudentCalc. Avoid entering other personal or sensitive information into a calculator.

Information collected when you visit

The current site does not provide sign-up, account, or calculator-submission forms. Its HTML and JavaScript do not include analytics, advertising, or other embedded third-party scripts, and do not intentionally read or write browser cookies or local/session storage. The site itself therefore does not receive calculator entries or create an analytics profile.

The website is hosted as static pages. GitHub Pages and any network, DNS, or content-delivery services that handle a request may automatically process technical information needed to deliver and protect the site, such as an IP address, requested page, browser or device details, and request time. These providers determine their own logs, retention, and any technical cookies or similar technologies; we do not control their infrastructure or retention schedules. Refer to the applicable provider privacy information for details.

Email messages

If you email us, we receive your email address and any information you choose to include. We use it to reply to your message or address the issue you reported. Email may be stored by the email service used to send and receive it. Please do not email passwords, student IDs, private academic records, or other sensitive information.

Cookies, advertising, and third-party services

There is no AdSense code or other advertising service in the current site files. If advertising is added, Google and other ad vendors used on the site may use cookies or similar technologies to serve, personalize, limit, and measure ads, prevent fraud, and understand ad interactions. Depending on the ad settings and location, ads may be personalized using activity on this or other sites, or may be non-personalized or contextual. Vendors may process information such as IP address, browser or device details, and ad interactions. Their collection and use are governed by their own policies.

When advertising is active, this policy should identify the vendors actually serving ads and link to their privacy and opt-out information. Google explains its ad technologies in Google's advertising privacy information. You can manage Google's personalized advertising in My Ad Center, review choices for some participating vendors at aboutads.info, and manage cookies through your browser. Blocking cookies or turning off personalization may not remove ads; contextual or non-personalized ads may still appear.

The current site does not load embedded third-party content. External links open third-party websites only when selected. If advertising or another third-party feature is introduced, applicable notices and consent choices will be provided where required by law or platform rules. No consent banner is currently used because no such third-party feature is enabled by the site's code.

External links

Our pages may link to third-party websites. Those websites operate independently and have their own privacy policies and practices. Review their policies when you visit them.

Children's privacy

StudentCalc is an educational resource that students of different ages may use. The site does not require visitors to provide personal information to use its calculators and does not knowingly collect children's personal information through the calculator tools. Please do not enter sensitive information into a calculator or send it by email. A parent or guardian with a privacy concern can contact us below.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the website or its services change. The date above shows when it was most recently revised. Questions about this policy can be sent to contact@kaflenischal1.com.np.
