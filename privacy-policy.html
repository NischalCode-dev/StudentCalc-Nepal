Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

StudentCalc Nepal is designed to work without an account. Calculator values are processed locally in your browser and are not submitted to a StudentCalc application server by this version of the site.

Information and site operation

The static website does not ask for personal information to use its calculators. Your browser or hosting provider may create standard technical logs, such as IP address, requested page, and timestamp, as part of delivering the site. The site currently does not include analytics, account tracking, or advertising code.

Cookies and third parties

This version does not intentionally set cookies. If third-party analytics, embedded services, or advertising (including Google AdSense) are added later, this policy must be updated to explain their data collection, cookies, opt-outs, and consent choices before those services are enabled.

External links

Links to third-party websites are provided for convenience. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

Children and contact

Do not send sensitive records or student identifiers through email. For a privacy question, contact contact@kaflenischal1.com.np.

Changes

This policy may be revised as the website changes. The date at the top indicates the latest revision.
